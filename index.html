Citadel AeroGrid™ Modular Windshaper

Developed in Turkey to overcome the static constraints of conventional wind tunnels. The Citadel AeroGrid utilizes a modular matrix of individually addressable fan pixels capable of generating arbitrary spatial and temporal wind conditions. Simulate urban gusts, ground-effect drafts, crosswinds, and rotor turbulence with precision.

  • Up to 25 m/s per wind pixel
  • Modular 25x25 cm 9-fan units
  • Python API & Web Control Interface
  • Open test volume for UAVs & VTOLs

Polymer Recycling & Size Reduction

Citadel ShredX-30 Single-Shaft Shredder

Engineered with a high-torque single rotary shaft with spiral staggered claw blades shearing against a rigid fixed counter-blade comb. Built on heavy-duty T-slot aluminum extrusion profiles and high-grade Hardox tool steel, the ShredX-30 crushes failed 3D prints, plastic purges, and engineering polymer waste into uniform 4mm regrind.

  • Single powered shaft & fixed counter-comb
  • 300 x 300 mm active cutting volume
  • Industrial planetary reduction gearbox
  • Auto-reverse jam clearance & E-stop safety

Citadel ExtrudeX Desktop Extruder

The premier laboratory desktop filament extruder designed to convert shredded plastics from the ShredX-30 or virgin pellets into certified 1.75 mm or 2.85 mm 3D printing filament. Featuring multi-zone PID heating bands, nitrided compression screw, optical dual-axis laser diameter micrometer with closed-loop speed adjustment, and motorized spool winding.

  • Optical dual-laser real-time diameter PID
  • Multi-zone 300°C ceramic heating barrel
  • PLA, PETG, ABS, PA-CF, TPU compatible
  • Integrated automatic spool winder & traverse

Quality Assurance & CE Standards Compliance

Machinery Directive Compliance

All Citadel equipment is engineered in accordance with international industrial standards:

  • CE Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC: Incorporating emergency shutoffs, physical guarding, dual interlocks, and risk minimization.
  • Low Voltage Directive 2014/35/EU: Electrical safety certification for high-torque motors, heating elements, and PSU racks.
  • EMC Directive 2014/30/EU: Electromagnetic compatibility across all brushless motor inverters, laser sensors, and digital bus communications.

ISO 9001 Quality Framework

Fabrication at our ODTU Teknokent partner facilities adheres to strict dimensional inspection protocols, laser hardness verification for shredder knives, and dynamic flow sensor calibration for our fan array wind systems.
